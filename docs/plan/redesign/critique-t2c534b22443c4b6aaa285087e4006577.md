## Findings

1. Three colour systems at once: theme.css (violet #8b5cf6 on #09090b), an older palette in live.css, settings.css and studio.css
   (#7c5cff, #12151d, #181c26, #eef0f6, #a9afc2), and progress.css (Notion blue and its own amber/red).
   settings.css hard-coded #181C26, #7C5CFF and #F5B544.
2. Gradients on titles, buttons, switches, segments, avatars, badges and progress fills. theme.css allows the gradient only on the logo and the 3 px active indicator.
3. No grid: radii 6/8/10/11/12/16, odd font sizes (10.5, 11.5, 12.5, 13.5), weights 560 and 650, transitions from 120 to 600 ms.
4. Duplicate UIs: a settings modal and a settings tab, two switch styles, two segmented controls.
5. Live is a 460 px overlay drawer, not a page in the shell.
6. Missing loading, empty, error and disabled states. White text on #8b5cf6 is only 4.2:1, so primary buttons use --nt-accent-press (#7c4deb).
7. Dead code: the 9 px "NotionTool" label on the composer border.

## Rules for the Settings pages (P5)

- Only theme.css tokens. Gradient 135deg #8b5cf6 to #22d3ee only on the logo and the 3 px active indicator.
- Type: Inter, 400/500/600/700; page title 20/28, section 16/24, body 14/20, small 13/20, meta 12/16, nothing under 12 px.
- Spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/48. Radius 6 (chips, segments), 8 (buttons, inputs, nav items), 12 (cards), 999 (pills, switches).
- Motion 120 ms for hover and focus, 200 ms for panels.
- Models are always named explicitly, never Auto. There was no role-model setting before P5; the keys are added in desktop_settings.py.
